# Phúc Bùi

Frontend developer, formerly leading the frontend team at ViiVue.

I care about getting things right, not just getting things done.
I work across frontend, motion, and AI - build open-source tools, and spend my spare time reading and learning languages.

Native in Vietnamese, professional in English, conversational in Mandarin, and Japanese just enough to be dangerous.


## Work

### Lipsum Generator

Built out of my own frustration. I generate lorem ipsum constantly when demoing layouts, so I made a Chrome extension
with exactly the features I kept wishing existed. 5,000+ weekly users apparently had the same frustration.

[→ View project](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/lipsum-generator/fepopmflofkppphpkfjdbmimglkeifna)

### Hiểu Chữ Hán

More than a dictionary. As a Vietnamese, I noticed that many Chinese characters share deep meaning with Vietnamese
through Sino-Vietnamese roots. This tool is built for that specific lens: helping Vietnamese learners understand and
remember each character from a familiar foundation, not from scratch.

[→ View project](https://hieuchuhan.phucbm.com)

### OpenWallet

Vietnam has a lot of credit cards and not enough clear comparison. I believe concentrated, structured data makes better
decisions possible. OpenWallet is an open-source card database for the Vietnamese market, helping users decide which
card to register, which to use for each purchase, and track their cards data-driven.

[→ View project](https://openwallet.vn)


## How I work

I'm not attached to tools. I'm attached to outcomes. Seven years of frontend work taught me that the right solution is
rarely the most obvious one, and that reaching for a new framework is usually the wrong answer. I build with whatever
gets the job done cleanly, and I'm comfortable enough with the fundamentals to know the difference.

Currently: Next.js, GSAP, TailwindCSS, Claude API, RAG, WordPress.

## Languages

Every language has one thing that bends your mind into thinking like a native speaker. Find that thing and the rest
unlocks faster. I write about learning Mandarin as a Vietnamese speaker, where the Sino-Vietnamese overlap gives us a
head start most people don't realize they have.
